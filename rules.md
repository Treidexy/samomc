# Rules

1. **Be respectful.** This is a student hosted server meant to have fun. Dont ruin this for us.
2. **No lag machines.**
3. **No cheating.** Hacked clients, X-ray.

## Etiquette

1. No griefing, stealing, killing etc
2. No PVP unless both parties consent (use `/pvp`)
3. No scamming ~~(use `/trade`)~~

## Space

1. To keep things simple, the world border is temporarily 5km in each direction.

## Mods/Datapacks

The Samohi SMP runs Minecraft 26.3 (Java & Bedrock) on Fabric with optimization mods that dont alter gameplay.

Here are some mods/datapacks that DO alter gameplay:

1. [`/tpa`](https://modrinth.com/mod/blossomtpa), [`/back`](https://modrinth.com/mod/blossomback) -- for easy meeting
2. [`/pvp`](https://modrinth.com/mod/blossompvp) -- to consent or unconsent to pvp
3. players_sleeping_percentage 0
4. [timber](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/datapacks/) -- cut down entire trees at once (hold shift to disable)
5. [armored elytra](https://modrinth.com/datapack/elytra-armor)
6. [more piglin safe armor](https://modrinth.com/datapack/more-piglin-safe-armor) -- netherite doesnt aggro piglins
7. [quick shulker boxes](https://modrinth.com/datapack/quick-shulker-boxes) -- hold shift and right click
8. [stackraft](https://modrinth.com/datapack/stackraft) -- condense materials like sugarcane or gunpowder
9. [quick undyingrefil](https://modrinth.com/datapack/undyingrefill) -- auto refil totems
10. [storm channeling](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/datapacks/) -- summon thunderstorm by trident up in air (uses a lot of duribility)
11. [villager death messages](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/datapacks/)
12. [copper powered rails](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/crafting-tweaks/)
13. [double shulker shells](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/datapacks/)
14. [straight to shapeless](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/crafting-tweaks/)
15. [universal dyeing](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/crafting-tweaks/)
16. [chunk loaders](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/datapacks/) -- drop nether star on loadstone

For a complete list see https://github.com/Treidexy/samomc/blob/main/README.md
